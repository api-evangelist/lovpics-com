LovPics is an AI visual production platform for eCommerce brands, D2C fashion labels, agencies, and sellers. It creates AI‑generated product photos, fashion shoots, banners, 360° visuals, videos, and marketing creatives without traditional photoshoots, offering studio‑grade exports up to 4K, multilingual support, and marketplace‑specific guardrails, promising up to 90% cost savings and 3‑minute turnaround.

**Areas:** Company · AI · Image-Generation · E-commerce · Marketing · SaaS

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for LovPics: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
